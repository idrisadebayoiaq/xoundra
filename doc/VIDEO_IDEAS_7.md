# Xoundra — 7 video ideas

Vertical video, about 15 to 25 seconds. One person, one room, the real app. No prices on screen. No claim that a sound cures insomnia or anything else. Xoundra is here for the night, the desk, or a loud mind.

End every caption with `Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app`.

Free includes listening. Premium covers the sleep schedule, Studio mixes, offline downloads, and the voice guide. Say that only when the video shows one of those four.

## 1. The chair

The day is over. They are tired in a chair, open Xoundra, put on headphones, and the shoulders drop.

Shot: a person drops into a chair, rubs their face, picks up the phone, opens Xoundra, plays Fine Rain or Rain on the Trees, puts on headphones, closes their eyes, and the face softens.

On-screen line: The day can end here.

Sound: rain, heard once the headphones go on.

Caption: Tired, and still in the chair. Open Xoundra, press play, and let the rain take the rest of the day. Find your quiet. Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app

## 2. Still awake

It is late. They cannot sleep. The phone stays dim. Rain starts. The phone goes face down.

Shot: a dark bedroom, the person on their side, screen low, Xoundra open on a rain sound, then the phone set face down on the bed. They settle.

On-screen line: Still awake? Rain is here.

Sound: Fine Rain or All-Night Rain.

Caption: Insomnia does not need another bright screen. Xoundra is here for the hours that will not settle. Put on rain, set the phone down, and let the room get quiet. Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app

## 3. It starts, then it stops

They set the sleep timer before bed. The sound will begin at night and end in the morning.

Shot: Profile, Sleep timer. They set 22:00 and 06:00, pick one rain sound, turn it on, put the phone on the bedside table, and turn off the lamp.

On-screen line: On at 10. Off at 6.

Sound: the chosen rain, only for a moment after they save.

Caption: You should not have to wake up to turn the sound off. Set the sleep timer, choose the rain, and leave the phone alone. The sound starts when you sleep and stops when you wake. Part of Premium. Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app

## 4. No words in the headphones

They are studying. A song with lyrics pulls them off the page. They switch to wind and keep working.

Shot: a desk, a notebook or laptop, they pull out an earbud, frown, open Xoundra, play Low Wind, put the earbud back, and return to the page.

On-screen line: No lyrics. Just the work.

Sound: Low Wind or another wind sound.

Caption: Words in the headphones are still words. Wind stays in the background for the hour you need to finish. Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app

## 5. Rain and a little wind

One sound is not enough. They build a small mix and sit back.

Shot: Studio. They add rain, add a little wind, lower one slider, press play, then lean back in the chair with headphones on.

On-screen line: Build the room you need.

Sound: the mix they just made.

Caption: Some nights need rain and a little wind together. Stack them in Studio, set how loud each one sits, and keep that room for next time. Part of Premium. Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app

## 6. The night away from home

They are in a strange room. The sound was downloaded earlier, so it plays with no signal.

Shot: a bag on a chair, they sit on the edge of a bed that is not their own, open Downloads, play a saved rain sound, and put on headphones.

On-screen line: The rain came with you.

Sound: a downloaded rain track.

Caption: A strange room should not decide if you sleep. Download the sound before you leave, then play it from the phone. The rain is already with you. Part of Premium. Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app

## 7. Say it out loud

They are too tired to browse. They tell the guide they cannot sleep. A sound starts.

Shot: dim room, person with the phone near their mouth, Guide open. They say they cannot sleep. The reply plays, a sound begins, they put on headphones and lie back.

On-screen line: You do not have to browse.

Sound: whatever the guide starts, then let it play.

Caption: Tell Xoundra the night you are having. The guide answers, then plays a sound that fits. Useful when choosing feels like too much. Part of Premium. Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app
