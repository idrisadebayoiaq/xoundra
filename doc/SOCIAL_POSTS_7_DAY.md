# Xoundra 7-day social plan

Version 1.1 · 19 October 2026

Seven days. Two posts each day: morning and evening. The morning post and the evening post are always different subjects, different photographs, and different headlines. They are never the same design resized.

Every X caption is under 280 characters, including the hashtag and the site. Instagram captions are the long version of the same post.

Every post ends with: `Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app`

The Android app downloads from that site. The same address also opens Xoundra in the browser.

## What the copy may claim

- Free includes listening, likes, favorites, and playlists.
- Premium adds Studio mixes, offline downloads, the sleep timer, and the voice guide.
- There are no ads on Free or Premium.
- Plans are Monthly, Annual, and Lifetime. Prices stay off these posts. They live on the Plans screen.
- After signup, the listener picks 5 sounds. Recommended starts from those picks.
- Artists can add a name, bio, cover, and sounds. Listeners can follow them and leave a note. These posts do not promise payouts.

## How each post is designed

Every post gets six files. Three different designs are made at TikTok size, 1080×1920. Those same three designs are then laid out again at Instagram size, 1080×1350. The Instagram file is the same photograph, headline, and screenshot, refitted so nothing is cropped. It is not a resized copy of the TikTok file.

Morning and evening never share a photograph or a headline.

Each design keeps the lines apart. The full welcome screen stays inside the phone, including Create account, Sign in, and Download app. The bottom bar does not cover the phone. Every design shows the Xoundra logo, a reason to try the app, `Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app`, `tiktok.com/xoundra.app`, and `x.com/xoundra_app`.

Files for a day live in `D:\PS\xoundra\day-0N-morning` and `D:\PS\xoundra\day-0N-evening`.

Day 1 is designed. Days 2 to 7 follow this same set of six files per post. Their headlines are listed with each day. The photographs change with the subject, and the phone shows the screen that matches that subject.
