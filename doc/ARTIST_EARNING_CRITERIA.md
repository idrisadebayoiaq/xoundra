# Artist earning criteria (draft)

Version 0.1 · 4 November 2026  
Status: **policy draft only** — no payouts or cash-out in the product yet.

## When payouts may open
1. Stable Premium revenue (subscriptions renewing reliably).
2. Clear play / completion attribution per artist (analytics already collect plays).
3. Separate Artist Terms accepted in-app.
4. Identity / tax path defined per country (later).

## Proposed eligibility (v1 when enabled)
- Verified / active artist (`profiles.is_artist`).
- At least **3** approved published tracks (or 1 album with 3+ tracks).
- No unresolved rights or community policy strikes in the last 90 days.
- Minimum **qualifying plays** in the payout month (threshold TBD after revenue data).
- Payout account details on file (bank / Paystack transfer — TBD).

## Payout model (v1 sketch)
- Monthly Creator Pool as a **percentage of Premium net** attributable to catalogue listening, or a fixed pool share.
- Paid only above a **minimum threshold**; balances roll forward.
- Quiet points stay **in-app** (download credit / Studio 24h). Real-money **points cash-out** stays deferred until payouts are trusted.

## Not in scope yet
- Advance payments, tips, or auctioned placements.
- Paying for Quiet Room posts or Bulletin membership.
