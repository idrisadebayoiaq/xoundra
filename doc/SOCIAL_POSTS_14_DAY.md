# Xoundra 14-day post plan

Two weeks. One post a day. Most posts are about what the sound does for the listener. A few posts show the tool that makes that easier.

Accounts: TikTok `tiktok.com/xoundra.app`, X `x.com/xoundra_app`.
Every post ends with `Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app`.

Free includes listening, likes, favorites, and playlists. There are no ads.
Premium adds the sleep schedule, Studio mixes, offline downloads, and the voice guide. Say “part of Premium” when a post is about one of those four. Leave prices off the posts.

Do not say a sound cures insomnia, anxiety, or anything else. Say it is here for the night, the desk, or the loud mind.
