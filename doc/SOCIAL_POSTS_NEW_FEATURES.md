# Xoundra new-features social plan

Version 1.0 · 3 November 2026

Fourteen days. **Two posts every day: one video, one image.**  
Morning = **video**. Evening = **image** (still + short caption set).  
Morning and evening never share the same hook, screenshot, or headline.

This plan covers what just shipped: Home/Discover/You redesign, Scenes, Evening, Quiet points, streak, continuous play, share cards, Guide sessions, Focus mode, PWA, weather hints, breath, sound DNA, packs, join-scene, check-ins, and the calmer Studio gallery.

Accounts: TikTok `tiktok.com/xoundra.app` · X `x.com/xoundra_app`  
Every post ends with: `Use the web version or download the app on xoundra.vercel.app`

---

## What the copy may claim

- Free includes listening, likes, favorites, and playlists.
- Premium adds Studio mixes, offline downloads, the sleep timer, and the voice guide.
- Free may show ads. Premium does not. Do not say “no ads” on Free.
- Quiet points: claim **+10 daily check-in** once per day; spend on **one download credit** or **Studio for 24 hours**. Do not invent other spends.
- Scenes are ready-made Studio mixes you can play or edit.
- Evening is a short wind-down ritual, not a medical sleep cure.
- Check-in emails are optional Good morning / Good evening notes. Push is optional in Settings.
- Plans stay off these posts. Prices live on the Plans screen.
- Do not say a sound cures insomnia, anxiety, or anything else.

## Design rules

**Video (morning)**  
15–25 seconds. Open on the benefit in under 2 seconds. Show the real screen. End on logo + site. Burn-in text stays short. Vertical 1080×1920 for TikTok / Reels / Shorts. Soft music under voice or ambient only — never drown the demo.

**Image (evening)**  
Six files when you design for static: three concepts at TikTok 1080×1920, same three refitted for Instagram 1080×1350 (not a resize crop). Logo, one headline, one reason, site, `tiktok.com/xoundra.app`, `x.com/xoundra_app`. Phone UI must be readable; bottom bar must not cover Create account / Sign in / Download when welcome is shown.

Files live under `D:\PS\xoundra\new\day-NN-video` and `D:\PS\xoundra\new\day-NN-image`.

**X** captions stay under 280 characters including the site and one hashtag when possible.  
**Instagram** uses the longer caption. Hashtags at the end only.

## Posting checklist (every day)

1. Film or export **morning video** (15–25s, vertical).  
2. Design **evening image** set (3 concepts × TikTok + Instagram if doing full static kit).  
3. Post video first (morning window for your audience).  
4. Post image in the evening — different hook.  
5. End every caption with the site line.  
6. Reply to comments with one calm sentence + the site if they ask where to get it.  
7. Do not promise medical outcomes, payouts, or Unlimited free Premium.
